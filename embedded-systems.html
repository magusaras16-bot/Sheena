Embedded Systems

Embedded systems are small computer systems made to do a specific job. They combine hardware and software to control how electronic devices work. These systems are designed to perform their tasks quickly and correctly.

Embedded systems are used in many everyday devices such as washing machines, microwave ovens, cars, medical equipment, and security systems. They help these devices work automatically and make them easier to use.

An embedded system usually has a microcontroller, sensors, memory, and software. These parts work together to receive information, process it, and produce the needed output. Embedded systems are an important part of modern technology and are used in many different industries.

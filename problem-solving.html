Problem Solving

Problem solving is the process of finding solutions to technical or practical challenges. It involves identifying the problem, understanding its cause, and choosing the best way to fix it.

In electronics and technology, problem solving is important for troubleshooting circuits, testing components, and improving system performance. It helps ensure that devices work safely and correctly.

Good problem-solving skills require careful observation, logical thinking, and patience. These skills are useful in designing, maintaining, and repairing electronic systems in different situations.

Networking

Networking is the process of connecting computers and devices so they can communicate and share information. It allows users to access files, applications, and internet services from different locations.

Networks are used in homes, schools, businesses, and many other places. They make communication faster, improve collaboration, and allow devices to exchange data quickly and securely.

A computer network is made up of devices such as routers, switches, cables, and wireless connections. These components work together to provide reliable communication and support the daily use of modern technology.
